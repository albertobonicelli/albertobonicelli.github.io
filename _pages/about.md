# Welcome to my personal webpage

I am a Postdoc in the group of stochastic analysis of Laboratoire de Probabilité, Statistique et Modélisation of the Sorbonne Université, led by [Prof. Lorenzo Zambotti](https://www.lpsm.paris/users/zambotti/index).
Prior to that, I was a PhD candidate in mathematical physics at the physics department of the University of Pavia, under the co-supervision of [Prof. Claudio Dappiaggi](https://claudiodappiaggi.com/) and [Prof. Nicolò Drago](https://nicolodrago.wixsite.com/mysite).

I completed my undergraduate studies at the University of Pavia, obtaining a BSc and an MSc in Physics summa cum laude.
